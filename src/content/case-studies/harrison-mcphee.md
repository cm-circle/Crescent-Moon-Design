## The Situation

Harrison McPhee is a third-generation arboriculture company serving the greater Boston area. They'd been in business since the early '90s, built entirely on word-of-mouth and a yellow-pages listing that stopped getting renewed around 2014. Their website was a single page on a dead hosting account — a logo, a phone number, and a photo of a truck that hadn't been updated since the Bush administration.

The problem wasn't that they were bad at their job. **They were arguably the best arborist in their service area.** But "best" doesn't matter if the person with the storm-damaged oak can't find you when they're Googling "tree removal near me" at 11pm on a Tuesday.

## What We Found

The audit revealed a mess that went deeper than "no website." Their business name was listed differently on six different directories (Harrison McPhee, Harrison McPhee Tree, H.M. Arboriculture, etc.). Their Google Business Profile was unclaimed. They had zero reviews online — not bad ones, just none. Their service area covered nine towns but there was no content mentioning any of them. Google had no reason to show them to anyone.

## What We Did

- Built a new five-page site from scratch: home, services, service areas, about, and contact. Hand-coded, no CMS, loads in 1.8 seconds on a mid-range Android over 4G.
- Created individual service-area pages for each of the nine towns they cover, with localized copy and schema markup for local business.
- Claimed and optimized their Google Business Profile: correct categories, service list, photos, hours, and a posting schedule.
- Audited and corrected their NAP (name, address, phone) across 40+ directories. Deleted duplicates. Fixed inconsistencies that had been confusing Google's local algorithm for years.
- Built a review generation system — a simple post-job text message asking happy customers to leave a review. Went from 0 to 47 reviews in five months.
- Wrote and published eight long-form articles targeting "how do I know if my tree is dying" type queries — the questions homeowners ask before they call an arborist.

## The Result

Within four months they were ranking #1 in the local pack for "arborist" and "tree removal" in three of their primary service towns. Organic traffic went from effectively zero to 2,800 sessions per month. The phone started ringing during the audit phase — before the site even launched — just from the Google Business Profile optimization and citation fixes.

By month six, they'd hired two additional crews. **They asked us to pause the SEO work because they physically couldn't take on more jobs.** That's a good problem to have.
